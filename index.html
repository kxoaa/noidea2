Orange

The breakfast classic. Bright, sunny and a little tangy.

  • TasteSweet citrus with a clean, tangy finish.
  • Best servedIce cold, first thing in the morning.
  • Goes withEggs, labneh sandwiches, croissants.

Mango

Thick, golden and made for summer afternoons.

  • TasteRich, tropical and smooth.
  • Best servedChilled, with a squeeze of lime if you like.
  • Goes withSpicy food, grilled chicken, shawarma.

Apple

Crisp and light. The easy one to reach for any time of day.

  • TasteFresh, mildly sweet, orchard-crisp.
  • Best servedVery cold, in a lunchbox or on the go.
  • Goes withSandwiches, biscuits, a quick snack.

Guava

Floral, fragrant and unmistakable. A favourite for a reason.

  • TasteSweet and perfumed, with a soft tartness.
  • Best servedCold, after a big meal.
  • Goes withDates, dessert, a long evening.

Pomegranate

Deep ruby red with a bold, tart bite.

  • TasteTart, juicy and a little earthy.
  • Best servedCold, in a tall glass.
  • Goes withRice dishes, grilled meat, salads.
